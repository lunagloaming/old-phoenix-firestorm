**Companion Viewer is a free client for 3D virtual worlds such as Second Life and various OpenSim worlds where users can create, connect and chat with others from around the world.**

## Based on Firestorm

Companion Viewer is derived from the [Firestorm](https://www.firestormviewer.org) viewer ([source](https://github.com/FirestormViewer/phoenix-firestorm)), release `Firestorm_Release_7.2.5.81788`, by The Phoenix Firestorm Project, Inc. Firestorm is itself derived from the official [Second Life](https://github.com/secondlife/viewer) client, whose codebase has been open source since 2007. Both are available under the LGPL license; see [doc/LICENSE-source.txt](doc/LICENSE-source.txt), [doc/LGPL-license.txt](doc/LGPL-license.txt) and [doc/LICENSE-logos.txt](doc/LICENSE-logos.txt).

Companion Viewer is not affiliated with or supported by the Firestorm project. Please do not take Companion Viewer issues to Firestorm's support channels.
